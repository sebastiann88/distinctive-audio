Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about the role of cables in your hi-fi system.

Do cables really matter? expand_more

Yes, but context is key. Cables don't "improve" sound; rather, poorly designed cables degrade the signal. A high-quality cable ensures that the fragile analog signal from your source reaches your amplifier, and ultimately your speakers, with minimal loss, phase shift, or interference.

How much should I budget? expand_more

A common guideline in high-end audio is to allocate roughly 10% to 15% of your total system cost to cabling and power conditioning. We prefer to evaluate on a case-by-case basis, ensuring your cable investment matches the resolving capability of your components.

Balanced (XLR) vs. Single-Ended (RCA)? expand_more

If your equipment features truly balanced circuitry, XLR cables are generally superior as they reject common-mode noise, especially over longer runs. However, for short runs between single-ended components, a high-quality RCA interconnect is often the purest path.

Can you make custom lengths? expand_more

Absolutely. In fact, we recommend it. We stock bulk cable and premium connectors to build interconnects and speaker cables to your exact room specifications, eliminating messy coils of excess wire behind your equipment rack.
