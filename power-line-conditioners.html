Frequently Asked Questions

What does a conditioner do? expand_more

It filters out electromagnetic and radio frequency interference from your AC line, providing a clean power foundation that lowers the noise floor of your entire system.

Will it limit my amp? expand_more

Modern high-quality conditioners and regenerators are designed to provide instantaneous peak current, often improving amplifier dynamics rather than restricting them.

Regeneration vs filtering? expand_more

Filters passively remove noise from the existing AC wave. Regenerators completely rebuild the AC wave from scratch, ensuring perfect voltage and negligible distortion.

Do I also need power cords? expand_more

Yes. While a conditioner cleans the power, high-quality AC cords ensure that power is delivered to your components without re-introducing noise or limiting current.
