Frequently Asked Questions

Expert guidance on bringing high-fidelity physical digital media playback into your system.

What is the difference between a player and a transport? expand_more

A CD player contains both the laser mechanism to read the disc and an internal Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) to output an analog signal directly to your amplifier. A CD transport only reads the disc and outputs a digital signal, requiring a separate external DAC to convert the signal before amplification.

Do I need a separate DAC? expand_more

If you purchase a CD transport, yes, you must use an external DAC (either standalone or built into your amplifier/preamplifier). If you purchase a standard CD player, it is not strictly necessary, but adding a high-quality external DAC later can often provide a significant sonic upgrade.

Is the Shanling CD player new or used? expand_more

The Shanling CD-T100 listed is a curated pre-owned model. We carefully inspect, test, and verify the performance of all our used equipment to ensure it meets our rigorous standards for playback quality and reliability.

How should I care for my discs? expand_more

Handle discs by the edges or center hole to avoid fingerprints. Store them in their cases away from direct sunlight and extreme heat. If cleaning is necessary, use a soft, lint-free cloth and wipe in a straight line from the center outward, never in a circular motion.
