FOUNDER & PRESIDENT

Rhéal Nadeau

DEC in analytical chemistry, BSc, MSc

Raised on a family dairy farm, Rhéal pursued a rigorous academic path culminating in three chemistry degrees. He spent 14 years working as a chemist and lab supervisor, developing a meticulous attention to detail and analytical rigor.

An avid audiophile since his college days, his passion for high-fidelity sound reproduction naturally evolved into a business. He founded Distinctive Audio part-time in February 1986, transitioning to full-time dedication by January 1988, and incorporated the business in Ontario in December 1999.

For nearly four decades, Rhéal has served the Ottawa–Gatineau community from the exact same location, applying his scientific background to the art of acoustic synergy.

About the Store

Established in 1986, Distinctive Audio has been providing expert service to our customers for nearly 40 years. We are a locally owned and operated business serving the Ottawa Gatineau region. Shopping at Distinctive Audio is a boutique-like experience where you will receive personalized service. Every customer’s needs are unique and addressed by offering specialized advice on our products. Through our expert advice we guide our customers through the process of selecting from our diverse inventory. Through this we help you build a truly satisfying high-end audio system. As a client of Distinctive Audio we will connect a high fidelity sound to the music you love. You will feel a greater emotional connection to your music.
