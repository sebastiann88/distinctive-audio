Frequently Asked Questions

Why a separate phono stage? expand_more

Built-in phono inputs on modern receivers are often an afterthought. A dedicated stage provides superior RIAA equalization, drastically lowers the noise floor, and ensures the delicate signal from your turntable is amplified with perfect linearity.

MM vs MC loading? expand_more

Moving Magnet (MM) cartridges typically require 47k ohms, while Moving Coil (MC) require specific, often much lower impedance loading to prevent ringing. A premium stage allows precise adjustment for flat frequency response.

Tube vs solid-state phono? expand_more

Solid-state designs excel at low noise floors and analytical detail, ideal for low-output MC cartridges. Tube designs offer euphonic second-order harmonics, creating a richer, more holographic soundstage prized by analog purists.

Matching gain to my cartridge. expand_more

Proper gain structure is critical. Too little gain results in a high noise floor when volume is raised; too much causes clipping and distortion. We assist in calculating the exact dB requirement for your specific cartridge output.
