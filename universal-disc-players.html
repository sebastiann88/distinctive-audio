What is a universal disc player, and do you need one?

A universal disc player spins virtually every physical format — CD, SACD, DVD-Audio, Blu-ray, and 4K UHD — through one audiophile-grade transport and DAC, so a single component covers both music and home theatre. Our Ottawa selection features the Magnetar UDP800 MKII at $3,499 and the reference-class UDP900 at $4,599.

Choose a universal player if you own SACDs or DVD-Audio discs, or want one machine for hi-fi listening and 4K video with balanced XLR outputs. For Red Book CD only, a dedicated CD player or transport may suit you better.

Frequently Asked Questions

What discs does it play? expand_more

Our universal disc players handle virtually every physical format, including CD, SACD (Super Audio CD), DVD-Audio, standard DVD, Blu-ray, and modern 4K UHD Blu-ray discs.

Does it do SACD? expand_more

Yes, these units feature dedicated DSD decoding for native SACD playback, ensuring you hear the high-resolution audio exactly as the mastering engineer intended, without down-sampling to PCM.

CD player vs universal? expand_more

While a dedicated CD player focuses purely on Red Book CD audio, a high-end universal player uses premium components (like dual power supplies and reference DACs) to provide audiophile-grade CD playback while adding the versatility to play SACD, DVD-A, and video formats.

Video output options? expand_more

They feature dual HDMI outputs, allowing you to send untouched video directly to your display while sending an isolated audio stream to your processor or receiver, minimizing interference.
